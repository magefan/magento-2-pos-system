## Why Use Magento 2 POS System?

Running separate online and offline stores without proper synchronization creates chaos for merchants. Inventory mismatches, delayed order updates, and manual sales tracking are just the tip of the iceberg that lead to poor customer experience and internal operational inconsistencies.

Without a dedicated Magento 2 POS system, merchants commonly face:

* Inventory discrepancies between online and physical stores
* Manual synchronization of orders, invoices, and refunds
* Slow checkout processes and long customer queues
Difficulties managing multiple locations and warehouses

Magefan Magento 2 POS System solves these issues by centralizing online and offline operations in one place. It helps merchants synchronize inventory, customers, and orders, and transform any device into a flexible point-of-sale terminal.

## Features of Magento 2 POS System
* Web-based Magento POS interface
* Multiple store locations, cashiers, and POS terminals
* Real-time inventory and order synchronization
* Barcode scanner integration
* Multiple payment methods support
* Self-checkout terminal support
* Z-Reports and X-Reports for sales tracking
* Hold shopping carts, apply discounts, and create custom products
* Responsive design for desktop, tablet, and mobile

## Benefits of the Magento POS System by Magefan
Magefan offers you a seamless integration of your brick-and-mortar store into the online ecosystem you're used to working with. It inherits all the Magento features like coupons, rules, inventory, special prices etc. to deliver a seamless experience across multiple location. 

### Track Data in Real Time

When managing a brick-and-mortar store, it’s important to keep all data in check: orders, invoices, refunds, and more. This Magento 2 POS system leverages the best Magento features to improve your POS experience. 

All POS sales data is tracked and stored in the Magento 2 admin panel. So, you can monitor terminals, create POS sessions, and track inventory and orders without leaving the backend.

No complex integrations or third-party add-ons. Only centralized POS management.

### Implement Multiple Payment Methods
  
The variety of payment options you offer has a direct impact on the customers' experience in your store. The Magefan Magento POS System has you fully covered by offering flexible [POS payment methods](https://magefan.com/magento-pos-system/payment-methods). 

It allows you to accept cash, credit cards, and Stripe payments. Besides, you can process POS orders via your regular Magento checkout and use all payment methods you have enabled for your online store.

This gives customers flexible checkout options and helps your team process sales faster.

This Magento POS System allows you to hold shopping carts without forcing customers to wait in line or scan each product from scratch. The customers can take their time and place their orders later, all while other customers can make their purchases uninterrupted.

### Keep Discounts and Promotions Consistent

For a fully synchronized experience, POS has to mirror your online store setup. With the Magefan’s POS System, your Magento discounts, cart, and catalogue price rules work in your POS, too.

Besides, cashiers can apply coupon codes to shopping carts when placing orders. This improves sales management and effectively coordinates your Magento and POS offers.

### Edit Product Price Directly in Cart

Magento allows you to set custom prices when you create orders from the admin panel. It is a great feature to create a personalized experience, and a nice-to-have for your POS system. 

This POS system for Magento from Magefan supports this feature out of the box. 

You can simply click on the product price in the cart and change it before processing the order. It allows you to check out customers with special prices without having to edit the product system-wide.

### Offer Custom Products (Custom Sale)

Sometimes, a cashier is not able to find a product in the POS system, or it simply hasn’t been added yet. But there is a willing customer waiting to place an order, and you have to find a solution. Fast.

This Magento 2 POS system allows you to create a [custom product](https://magefan.com/magento-pos-system/custom-product) or a custom sale on the spot. Just specify the title, price, and quantity of the product you want to sell, and figure out why the item is missing later. This way, you do not lose sales and ensure a smooth purchasing journey.

### Enable Self-Checkout Terminal

Waiting in line is not something every customer has time or desire to do. This is a direct way to lose sales, especially in busy stores when checking out takes longer than finding the necessary product.

A solution? Offer a [self-checkout terminal](https://magefan.com/magento-pos-system/self-checkout) to let customers complete their purchases on their own. With this POS system for Magento, you can add it with no complex integrations. Just a few configuration options let you enable another more convenient checkout option.

### Manage Products in Multiple Warehouses

If you have a complicated network of stores and warehouses, you might be wondering if the POS system can support it. This is not a problem for the Magento 2 POS System, as it is fully compatible with multiple warehouses.

Thus, you can easily monitor products between different locations, see the product quantity in each of them, and coordinate the sales more effectively. So, if you don’t have a specific product in your store, you can always direct your customer to the other location, where they’ll find what they are looking for.  

### Keep Track of Cash Operations
To effectively manage POS sales, it’s crucial to keep track of all the cash operations during the day. The Magento 2 POS System has this aspect covered as well. 

It logs all instances of putting in or withdrawing the cash, along with the time stamp, the responsible cashier, and a comment. This helps you monitor the cash flows and keep this aspect in check.

### Integrate a Barcode Reader
While cashiers can find products by their names, SKUs, or other attributes, this process can be optimized even more. You can integrate the barcode reader to add products to the cart faster and offer a better customer experience. 

There’s no complicated setup. You just need to connect the barcode reader and choose which attribute represents the barcode. Having done that, your cashiers will be able to process purchases much more easily and quickly. 

### Cover All Your Selling Points
If you have multiple selling points, you have to ensure all of them are represented in your POS for better consistency and management.

This Magento POS System allows you to create as many locations as needed, along with the terminals and cashiers. This way, you create a clear structure of your stores and sales staff, and ensure that the sessions are running smoothly for each location. 

### Generate X and Z Reports
Each of your cashiers starts a session when clocking in. During the day, the corresponding terminals serve different types of operations, which can be tracked thanks to the X and Z reports.

The Magento 2 POS System supports both types of reports. All your cashiers have to do is start and then close the session. The detailed reports will be available in the Magento dashboard, featuring the store name, location, cashier, opening/closing balance, cash put in/withdrawn, and more. 

### Modify POS Receipts
Every little detail leaves a lasting impression on customers: communication with the sales agents, availability of the items, the convenience of placing an order, etc. POS receipts are important, too. 

Since they are part of your branding, this Magento POS System allows you to add your logo and custom text to the receipts. This makes them feel like a natural part of your store and adds to the general impression customers receive when visiting.

### Use any Device as a Retail Tool
One of the biggest perks of the Magefan Magento POS System is its responsiveness. Your team can use any device — desktop, tablet, or mobile — to process orders and assist customers. 

This way, you are not limited to a single device and can place orders from any place in your store. You get much more flexibility and can offer a seamless experience for your customers.

## Frequently Asked Questions about Magefan POS System for Magento

### What is the Magento 2 POS System?

Magento 2 POS system is a point-of-sale extension that connects physical store sales with a Magento store in real time. It synchronizes orders, inventory, customers, and payments inside Magento so merchants manage all channels from one admin panel. For example, when a cashier sells an item in-store, stock is updated immediately online and across warehouse locations.

### Can Magento POS work on tablets and mobile devices?

Magefan Magento 2 POS System is fully responsive and works on desktops, tablets, and mobile devices. This allows sales agents to complete purchases anywhere in the store.

### Can Magento POS use the same payment methods as in our online store?
Magefan Magento 2 POS System offers a dedicated payment method, Magefan POS Magento Payment. It allows you to process POS transactions via your regular Magefan checkout and use the same payment options you have enabled in your online store.

### What hardware does Magento 2 POS support?
The Magento 2 POS by Magefan supports all the necessary hardware devices: terminal, cash drawer, barcode reader, credit card reader, and receipt printer. If you need to integrate any other hardware, you are always welcome to contact us and explore possible solutions.
